Privacy policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

Information we collect

When you contact Good Tap Water, we collect what you give us: your name, phone number, email (if you share it), ZIP code, property type, water source and water concerns.

How we use it

We use this information to answer your request, talk about water-treatment options and set up a consultation. Contacting us does not sign you up for marketing messages.

Storage and service providers

Requests sent through our website form are delivered to our team by a form service and may be emailed to us. Our web host and related providers may process the data needed to run this website.

Text messages

If you text us, your message and phone number are handled by our phone carrier and stay with our team. We only use them to reply to you.

Online appointments

If you book through our online calendar (Calendly), Calendly's own privacy policy applies to the information you enter there.

Your choices

To see or delete a request you sent us, call or text (386) 387-8505. We'll take care of it, subject to any records we're required to keep.
